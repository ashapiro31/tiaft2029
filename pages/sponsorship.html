Why TIAFT 2029?

The 2029 meeting of The International Association of Forensic Toxicologists (TIAFT) will bring scientists and professionals working in forensic toxicology to Vancouver. The meeting provides sponsors and exhibitors with an opportunity to engage with a specialized community working in areas such as postmortem toxicology, drug- and alcohol-related impairment, forensic drug testing, and the interpretation of toxicological findings.

1

Engage with forensic toxicologists and other professionals from Canada and around the world.

Showcase your organization, products, services or commitment to forensic science to a highly specialized audience.

3

Support the science

Help advance scientific knowledge, professional exchange and collaboration across the forensic toxicology community.

Who should consider sponsorship?

TIAFT 2029 welcomes partners whose work connects with forensic science, toxicology, public safety, health, justice or laboratory practice.

  • Analytical instrumentation and laboratory technology
  • Diagnostic and laboratory suppliers
  • Pharmaceutical and biotechnology organizations
  • Government and public agencies
  • Police and public-safety organizations
  • Automobile, disability and life insurers
  • Road-safety organizations
  • Legal and forensic services
  • Scientific publishers and professional organizations
  • Other organizations supporting forensic science
